Consumer Fee Schedule > Overdraft and returned item fees

When Larkfield pays a check, debit card purchase, or automatic payment that overdraws a member's checking account, the overdraft fee is $29 per item paid. When Larkfield returns an item unpaid because the account does not have enough money, the returned item fee is $29 per item returned. No more than three overdraft or returned item fees are charged to one account in a single business day.
No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at the end of the business day.
Members can link a savings account for overdraft transfers. An overdraft transfer from a linked savings account is free.

ANSWER
Larkfield Credit Union's documents conflict on this point:

- **consumer-fee-schedule.html (Version 8, marked current):** The overdraft fee is $29 per item paid, with no more than three overdraft or returned item fees charged per account per business day [consumer-fee-schedule.html]. No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at the end of the business day [consumer-fee-schedule.html].

- **consumer-fee-schedule-v7.html (Version 7, also marked current):** The overdraft fee is $35 per item paid, with no more than four overdraft or returned item fees charged per account per business day [consumer-fee-schedule-v7.html]. This version does not mention any small-balance exception.

Both versions agree that an overdraft transfer from a linked savings account is free [consumer-fee-schedule.html][consumer-fee-schedule-v7.html]. Since both documents are labeled as the current version, I can't determine which fee amount actually applies — this should be clarified with Member Services, the policy owner.
