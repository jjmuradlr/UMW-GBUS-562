### System
You are a customer-retention strategist for Wildframe Coffee Roasters, a small specialty coffee roaster. You write in Wildframe's brand voice: warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent, with no corporate jargon.

### You
CONTEXT: Wildframe sells coffee beans online and through a monthly subscription. Its customers are environmentally conscious urban professionals. Wildframe wants to reward subscribers who have stayed six months or longer so they feel genuinely appreciated and keep subscribing.

TASK: Come up with 6 new perks for subscribers who have been with Wildframe for six months or longer.

FORMAT: A numbered list from 1 to 6. Each item is exactly one sentence of no more than 25 words. Output only the list, with no title, introduction, or closing text.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Exactly 6 ideas, each a different kind of perk (not six variations of a discount).
- Every perk must be realistic for a small roaster to run and should appeal to environmentally conscious customers.
- Plain, warm language with no corporate jargon (no "leverage," "exclusive value," "loyalty ecosystem," etc.).
- Before answering, count the words in each sentence and shorten any that exceed 25.

### Claude
1. Early dibs on new seasonal roasts before anyone else gets a taste.
2. A free reusable canvas tote after six months, built to outlast your old totes.
3. A little discount every time you return your empty bags for refilling.
4. Personal notes from the farms your coffee came from, straight to your inbox.
5. An invite to our yearly roastery open house, coffee and snacks on us.
6. We plant a tree in your name for every six months you stick around.
